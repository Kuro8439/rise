What We Provide

Services & Amenities

Every resident receives structured daily support in a safe, respectful, home-like environment built for adult mental health recovery.

Living & Daily Care

  • Completely Furnished Living Spaces
  • Three Healthy Meals Served Daily & Snacks
  • Assistance with Laundry & Housekeeping
  • High-Speed Wireless Fidelity & Cable Television

Health Oversight & Support

  • Vital Signs Monitoring & Health Tracking
  • Structured Medication Reminders & Tracking
  • Emotional Support & 24/7 On-Site Staff Supervision
  • Care Coordination with Outside Healthcare Providers

Daily Support & Community

  • Light Meal Preparation & Dietary Accommodation
  • Transportation & Scheduling for Medical Appointments
  • Safe, Structured & Respectful Community Environment
  • Designated Outdoor Relaxation Areas
  • Therapeutic Art & Cognitive Enrichment — guided drawing, painting, brain-stimulation puzzles, adult coloring, and creative expression for emotional relaxation and mental clarity
